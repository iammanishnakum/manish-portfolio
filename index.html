Who I Am

Data Science undergraduate with experience in student representation, event coordination, and hands-on technology projects.

I'm Manish Nakum, pursuing a B.Tech in Data Science Engineering at Dr. B. R. Ambedkar National Institute of Technology Jalandhar. My campus roles have involved representing students, coordinating teams, managing logistics, and helping events run smoothly.
